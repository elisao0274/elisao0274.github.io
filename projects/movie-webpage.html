Movie Webpage Project

Content

We put all of CSS and HTML into one single project in order for us to comprehend the skills we've memorized during SEP. This is a step of memorization and for us to learn more about our mistakes and expand our knowledge to do better on a next project

During the process of this, me and my partner had the free decision to choose any topic or genre we wanted, and we landed on action. We then decided to reference the anime "Attack on Titans". The purpose was for us to give the audience the aftermath of when Attack on Titans ended, our own scenario. Me and my partner added vocabulary that could gain the readers attention, interesting context and also images so they can visualize.
